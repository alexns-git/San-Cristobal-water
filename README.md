# San Cristóbal: water and sewage analysis · análisis del agua y el drenaje

An interactive map and explainer for the water system of San Cristóbal de las Casas, Chiapas, Mexico: rainfall, rivers and springs, pumping, delivery to homes, sewage, the drainage tunnel and the land below it.

Open the page: https://alexns-git.github.io/San-Cristobal-water/

The page is in English and Spanish and follows the language of the visitor’s device; the EN/ES switch at the top changes it, and `?lang=es` or `?lang=en` in the address opens it in that language.

It is a single self-contained file, `index.html`. Sources and notes on the data are listed at the foot of the page.

Data credits: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Contours, neighbourhood outlines and census coverage from the Chiapas state statistical and geographic information committee (CEIEG). Protected-area boundary from CONANP/CONABIO. Terrain heights from NASA SRTM.
